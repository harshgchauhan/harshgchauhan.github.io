AMAZON AMBASSADOR

the guy they send the new hires to.

Appointed · Training & Safety
Amazon DBZ4 Kitzingen, Germany
In addition to RTS Associate — late shift debrief

Picked to train the floor.

At Amazon, Ambassador isn't a marketing title — it's the associate leadership picks to train everyone else. I was appointed at DBZ4 Kitzingen to onboard new hires: the debrief and RTS process, the SOPs behind it, and the safety standards that keep a shift running.

The appointment is earned, not applied for. You get it when shift leadership trusts your process knowledge and your safety record enough to put new associates in your hands — and to let your sign-off decide when someone is ready to run the process alone.

Mine is the combined version: process training and safety coaching in one role, which sits directly on top of my Certified Fire Marshal qualification and TDR authorization.

what it actually involves ↓
  • Onboarding new associates on the RTS and debrief process, and Amazon SOPs
  • Shadowing new starters through first shifts until they can run it solo
  • Coaching safe working practice, hazard awareness, and correct equipment use
  • First point of contact on the floor for process questions
  • Correcting process deviations in real time to hold quality standards
  • Signing off new hires as competent to work independently
